During this event, monsters around your level will have a chance to drop shamrocks and green Leprechaun Boxes. The shamrocks will go into the material tab of inventory. The boxes will contain one part of the Leprechaun costume set and initially they go into consumables, where they will stack.  When you open them, the costume pieces will be in the equipment tab.
Note: the costume will become account bound when you equip it.

There is also a repeatable event quest. Start by talking to Judy in Zant. She will ask you to gather 10 shamrocks. These are the shamrocks that you might already have in your material inventory.
 
When you return to Judy, she will trade your 10 shamrocks for 10 nets... to catch the leprechauns with of course     The quest is called "The Leprechauns Run Amok" and it shows in your quest log.  Put the nets on your skill bar, along with the "Target Enemy" skill. When you kill a mob and a leprechaun appears, click on the skill right away, then try throwing the net. These little guys are really fast but they always run away from you, then back towards you, so soon enough you will figure out their pattern. 

Go back to Judy when you gather 10 proofs and you will get the following options
