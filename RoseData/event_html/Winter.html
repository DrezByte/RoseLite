Evanna
Lunaris RPS (Ruper-Penguin-Slag)
Cost – 75k
Reward- 5 festival tickets if you win; 1 ticket for a loss or a draw.

This is a version of rock-paper-scissors. You pick a pet and that pet goes up against the NPC’s pet. Rupers win Slags, Penguins win Rupers, Slags win Penguins. Ofc you never know which pet the NPC will pick


Chilly
Snowmobile Races
Cost 100 festival tickets to get the Snowmobile Boots, which have to be equipped, to do the race. You also need a snowmobile.

Equip Snowmobile Boots, get on your snowmobile, and try to be the fastest around the track! You are given 1:20 to go one lap. Make sure you pass all the checkpoints! The boots are yours to keep forever.

Reward: 1 festival ticket for each successful lap completed within the time. NOTE: The festival tickets go directly into your inventory as you complete each lap.  You don't need to speak to the NPC again.

Reward 4 festival tickets for 2nd place 
Reward 3 festival tickets for 3rd place 
Reward-2 festival tickets for 4th place

You will get a penguin in your consumables. When you double click on it, it’s “released” and you will see which ribbon you got. You really don't have to move from the NPC. Just release the penguin then turn in your ribbon for the reward


Val
Ice Fishing
Cost 50k
Reward 1 festival ticket for every fish caught

Look in your basic skill tab for the skill called "Target Enemy" I'd suggest putting in on your skill bar. Next find the 10 nets in your consumables and also put them on your skill bar. Then go into the water and stand near some fish. Double click on the target enemy skill until you see a fish targeted (you will see the red HP bar). Double click on the net and you will either get the fish or you'll miss. The fish themselves seem to disappear fast so work quickly.


Mighail
Lunaris Bear Throw
Cost 225k
Reward 0 hits- 4 tickets
1 hit- 10 tickets
2 hits- 15 tickets
3 hits- 20 tickets

You can use the same tactics as in the Ice Fishing game only you will be throwing fluffy snowballs instead of nets.


Viktoria
Snow Valley Biathlon
Cost 375k
Reward 90 festival tickets for 9 hits

You need a Biathlon Rifle to participate, which the NPC will sell to you for 450 Festival Tickets.

Equip the rifle then go to the set of three targets right next to Viktoria. Click on each target (nothing will happen) or use the "Target Enemy" skill, then double click on the stack of 12 bullets in your consumables. Usually I put the bullets on my skill bar. When you have hit each target once, the words "Stage Completed" will appear. Then move on to the next set of three targets and repeat. All three areas of three targets are along that perimeter road around the map. You will get 10 tickets for each hit up to 90 tickets total per game. Here are the other two locations. NOTE: You will get to keep the rifle so you don't have to buy another one next year.

Besides the festival in Snow Valley, there is something happening on Luna Planet too. Ice Chouchous!
These event drops look like the ice shards. They will drop from the monsters on Luna. You can exchange the Ice Chouchous for event items. The Ice Chouchous will lock to the character that gets them but the event items are all tradable until equipped.

Once you have some Ice Chouchous, talk to Eliot on the lower level of the City of Eucar on Luna. He is the guy that “taught” you the language when you came to Luna for the first time. He will offer to exchange the Ice Chouchous for a snowboard set. It will take 380 Ice Chouchous to get the set

Spoiler

Remember it's best not to have more than one stack of the event drop in your inventory when you speak to the NPC

 

 

Once you have the Snowboard Set, equip it on the avatar (not costume) then talk to Eliot again with some Ice Chouchous in your inventory. He still offers to exchange them for the snowboard set, but now you can also get a couple of snowboards and 2 pet capsules.  NOTE: The pet capsules are character locked.

Spoiler

Again, be sure not to have more than one stack of Ice Chouchous in your inventory when you talk to him.

The female's snowboards have a Braunite Trilliant [13] to show gem placement.
