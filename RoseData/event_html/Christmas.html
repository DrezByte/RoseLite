It's Christmas time in ROSE!  There is a lot to do in this event. Monsters will sometimes drop Christmas Presents that contain HP and MP items and some seasonal things. The Christmas Presents will go into the consumable tab so be sure you have space there.  Snow Crystals will also drop from monsters around your level.  Snow Crystals will go into the material tab. Unlike many event drops, the Snow Crystals will lock to the character that picks them up so if you have drop sharing turned on in your party, make sure that everyone manually picks up a few. [Event Info] Judy in Zant
Santa has his own planet and Judy in Zant will teleport you there for the price of one Snow Crystal. You need one Snow Crystal every time you go there.   To return to Zant, talk to [Event Info] Loelsch, the NPC next to Santa.


Santa Planetoid
In addition to dropping Snow Crystals, monsters on Santa's Planet will also drop Elifedora.  These will also go into the consumable tab so be sure you have space.  The Evil Reindeer on the Santa Planetoid do not give experience but it is fun to follow them around attacking them since they don't fight back.

Snow Crystals can be farmed on this planet as well as on the regular maps but Elifedora can only be farmed on the Santa Planet. Elifedora will be in your consumable tab. When you have at least one Elifedora and 10 Snow Crystals, talk to Santa and he will trade them for pieces of the Red Santa Suit. The costs are listed here.  NPC's get confused easily so when you want to exchange Snow Crystals and Elifedora for event items, be sure you do NOT have more than one stack of each.  Put the rest of them into storage before talking to Santa

Santa now has Rudolph Choice Boxes where you can pick from the 9 new Rudolph Set pieces



The Rudolph Gift Boxes are choice boxes. For those who are not familiar with choice boxes, just move the arrow from 0 to 1 on whichever piece you want. There will be a confirmation popup. Double check to be sure this is what you want, then confirm. If you change your mind before confirming, click on the arrow to change the 1 to 0 again. The choice boxes will be in your consumable tab, but the costume pieces inside will go to the equipment tab so before you open any boxes, make sure that you have space,

Bob the Evil Reindeer  
Bob is in the middle of this map.



His dialog is pretty funny. Be sure to keep him talking about how much he hates Santa.  Eventually, he will give you  the "Bob The Evil Reindeer" quest. It's pretty simple- find 20 stolen presents. They are scattered around near NPC's on Junon, Luna and Eldeon Planets. You can tell there is a present by the swarm of fairy lights. Here is an example. Each time you click on one of these, you will have "stolen" it. You're also rewarded with a Small Christmas Present.  See the first spoiler in this topic for information on what is inside those. 



When you have collected 20 stolen presents, return to Bob and he will give you a box that contains one piece of the Black Santa Suit. Unopened the box will go into consumables. When opened, the item will be in equipment. Which part you get is random, but the parts are tradable and become account bound when equipped. These pieces may also have substats.

[Mayor] Darren
Santa's sleigh crashed in Adventurer's Plains and the goblins stole it and all of the reindeer! Darren wants you to go knock some sense into them until they confess. This begins the quest called "Here We Come A-Wassailing".   Kill goblins in Goblin Cave until you get the quest drop, then return to Darren.  

The next part is called "Joy to the World".  Put the candy cane Darren gives you on your skill bar and go back to Goblin Cave. Kill goblins until a reindeer appears. Click on the reindeer, then on the candy cane. The number you need is 9- no more and no less.


The quest says to lead them out one at a time. That's not technically true- you don't physically lead them (actually they disappear lol)  When you "get" a reindeer, you will see a picture in your quest log called "enticed" reindeer. The icon has a red ! near the reindeer's face. It's kind of difficult to see because it's under the number 1.


When you leave Goblin Cave and re-enter, the reindeers you already have change from "enticed " to "saved".


Saved reindeer can not be lost, so my advice is to leave and re-enter when you have 2, maybe 3 at the most.  You can go to the entrance to Goblin Cave to save them if you don't want to actually exit. The exclamation point changes from red to green for the saved ones. This ss shows a saved and an enticed reindeer.


If you try to entice too many, you may suddenly see a lot of reindeer with this message.  This means that you've lost those enticed ones. You can try to get them back before they disappear but chances are that some will be gone before you can recapture them all.


When you have saved 9 reindeer, return to Darren for your reward- 100 snowballs and a Silver Whistle Box.


Click on it in your consumables tab and you will summon your very own reindeer mount! The mount will lock to your character when you use it. This quest is not repeatable.
