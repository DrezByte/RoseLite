Love is in the air in ROSE!
During this event, monsters around your level will drop Magic Heart Candies. Be sure to keep those because you can exchange them for some neat event items.
Head over to Adv Plains and talk to [Ambassador of Love] Qpid. You can't miss him- he is in the middle of the little village when you first enter the game.

Spoiler



 

He gives a quest called "Magic Heart Candy Express".  When you double click on the icon in the quest log, it opens the list of NPC's that you have to talk to. You must have some Magic Heart Candies in your inventory before they will have the quest dialog.  The quest tracker will help but this is what the quest log will show

NOTE: You must be able to travel to Eldeon to take and complete this quest. 

Spoiler

Here is the list of NPC's and what they will be asking for. You need 650 total candies to complete this quest

[Village Chief] Gray in Adv Plains – You need 20 candies. and he gives you a Firecracker Crate

[Guide] Lena in Zant- You need 100 candies and she gives you  Public Display of Affection Pack

[Akram Ambassador] Eliot in Eucar- You need 130 candies and he gives you Power Of Love (4)

[Chef] Peppie on Eldeon- You need 400 candies and you will get Heart Ammo Crate 

Return to Qpid after you complete the quest and he will give you Qpid's Bow 
 

The rewards are pretty cute. Check in the spoiler below to see them
