There is a lot going on during this event. Let's start with the drops.  Ancient Pirate Coins will drop from monsters around your level. They can be exchanged for some event items and other things by talking to Captain Peg Leg. You'll find him at the back of Junon Polis.

Pirate Treasure Chests can also drop from monsters.  They will stack in the consumable tab of your inventory and will contain one of the following: 
Pirate Booty Box
Ancient Pirate Coin
Pirate Treasure Box 

MAKE SURE YOU HAVE SPACE IN YOUR INVENTORY TABS BEFORE YOU START OPENING THESE CHESTS AND BOXES

Option 7 "I'd like to hunt for the Captain Pirate Thief Bug" 
Option 8 "Where can I find Captain Pirate Thief Bug and his crew?"
Option 9 :I have defeated Captain Pirate Thief Bug"
These three options are all related  to the same quest. 

Spoiler

You must be able to travel to Orlo Planet (level 200 (180 if using the portal in OT)) in order to do this quest.
This quest has several parts, but you don't have to return to Junon every time.  As you complete one part, the quest name and objective both change so you can just continue until you're done with all four of them, then return to Captain Peg Leg to claim your reward.

"In search of Captain Pirate Thief Bug (1)"
The first part of the quest requires you to find and kill 5 Third Mate Pirate Thief Bugs.  All of these mates will spawn randomly while fighting monsters on Orlo. They are pretty small and very fast, so when you see one, go kill it as quickly as you can.  

The Third Mate Pirate Thief Bugs will spawn on the Golden Ring map.  No need to post locations since they can spawn from killing any monster on the map, but here is a ss to give you an idea of what they look like.


When you have defeated 5 of these little guys,  the quest name will change.

"In search of Captain Pirate Thief Bug (2)"
Now you need to defeat four Second Mate Pirate Thief Bugs. You'll find them on Wasteland Ruins Path and it works the same way.  Kill monsters on that map to spawn them.

"In search of Captain Pirate Thief Bug (3)"
Next is the First Mate Thief Bug. Defeat two of these buggers.   These will spawn on either of the Wasteland Maps and it works like it did on the other maps.

"In search of Captain Pirate Thief Bug (4)"
The final part of this quest is to defeat Captain Thief Bug. Unlike his mates, this guy can be found in the Desert City of Muris and he is larger than the other bugs were and as you can see, he has a yellow name even for a level 250 toon.  You must be in a party to get the quest proof for killing the Captain Pirate Thief Bug.


Return to Junon and choose the last option “I have defeated Captain Pirate Thief Bug”. He will ask if you want to give him 15 Ancient Pirate Coins, which you must have in your inventory already. When you agree, he will take the coins and give you the Pirate Frigate. There are several different colors of this cart frame in the Locked Pirate Frigate boxes, but the only way to get the one that's just called Pirate Frigate is by completing this quest. The quest is repeatable
