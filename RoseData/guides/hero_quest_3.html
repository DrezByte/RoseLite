Spoiler

The Fairy's Rhyme (1)
You must be level 70 and have unlocked interplanetary travel in order to continue with the quest. Information on how to do that can be found here.


[Akram Ambassador] Eliot is on the lower level of the Magic City of Eucar on the Luna Planet. If this is your first time visiting Luna, Eliot will first teach you the "language". This is important so that you can understand what the NPC's on Luna are saying.


The Fairy's Rhyme (2)

The Fairy's Rhyme (3)


In between Eliot and Ishtal on the lower level of Eucar, there is a ramp leading outside of the city. Exit the city and turn to the right.  Right in front of the entrance to Mana Snowfields, there is an area with wolves.




Once you have the wolf pelts, return to Eucar and take one of the two ramps to the second level of the city.  In the center of the upper level, there is a portal.  Before you step into that field warp, head over to [Tavern Owner] Anzhelika  and buy some Eucar Return Scrolls. You can also check player's shops to possibly find them cheaper.

Now return to the portal and choose option 4 Crystal Snowfields south

[Shamanist] Est is the NPC that will teleport you into Luna Clan Field.

DARK RELIC

Spoiler

Dark Relic (1)


Dark Relic (2)



Go to the portal on the upper level of Eucar and pick option 4.  You will spawn near [Shamanist] Est.  Follow the wall on the right side until you see this small bridge. The Dreadnaughts are across that bridge. You can kill the Junior Dreadnaughts to get these quest drops. NOTE There are Dreadnaughts scattered around the area near Luna Clan Field if you prefer to hunt them there instead.


When you finish with the Dreadnaughts, head back to Est  Continue past the NPC and clan field and keep going. There are Mammoths all along that area and you can kill, Mammoths or Elder Mammoths. A good concentration of them can be found here.


To find the Vulcans, continue along this path until you see two paths.  Keep moving to the right.


Vulcans look like walking candy corn.  Enter the enclosed area to find more of them.  Both Vulcans and Vulcan Leaders can give the quest drops.


When you have gathered everything, go back to Eliot in Eucar.

ACTIVATING THE MANA LENS

Spoiler

Activating the Mana Lens (1)


Head back to Mana Snowfields (Eucar, lower level and turn right). Once inside Mana Snowfields, go straight. Again, you can take either path when the road splits.  Your destination initially looks like a block of ice but way at the top, you can see part of an NPC's name and the very tip of the Crystalline structure.


Before you worry about that, you need to gather some essence from the Igloo Turtles. Walk around until you find an opening and once inside, you will see the Igloo Turtles. NOTE: You can get the essences from either the Igloo Turtles or the smaller Igloo Tangles.




When you've gathered the essences, the quest tracker changes and says "Speak to Kiroro".  That's the NPC whose name was peeking out from the top of this structure so head up the winding path until you reach the top. There are Penguin Commanders and a Penguin King up there too. 


While talking to Kiroro, suddenly Melendino breaks in and you are forced to defeat her.  Talk to Kiroro again and find out the true story.  After she helps you activate the Mana Lens, hurry back to Eucar and talk to Olleck.

THE ORACLE OF ICE

Spoiler

The Oracle of Ice


To find the Bebegs, you have to continue going up. From where the Prince is, walk until you see this ice ramp across from the giant skull.


The next ramp is visible from the one that you just climbed so head up that ramp now


From the top of that second ramp, turn to the left, You can see another ramp going up. Remember this ramp but don't go on it. Instead go into the pond on the left side of that ramp to find the Bebegs. There are several small ponds in that area so go in a bit more to find a better spawn.

 
NOTE: Make sure that you have one space in the consumable tab of your inventory before you start this part of the quest.

The ramp turns into an icy stairway. Climb to the very top and double click on the Oracle of Ice.


The Goddess Luna explains what happened and that she won't be able to help because she is still trapped in the Sea of Dawn. Arua's Fairy pops in after that and tells you to head to Eldeon.
