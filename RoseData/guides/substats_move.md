# [Guide] How to Move Substats

This is a very neat feature that was added to the game a few years before NA closed. The concept is a bit confusing, especially for new or returning players. Everything here is in reference to stats on gear only (weapons, armor, accessories, etc.).

## Terms

**BASE STAT** — always in bold blue text. An item may have one base stat, two, or none. Base stats can't be removed. `[IMAGE 1 — base stat Max HP 30]`

**SUBSTATS** — in lighter blue text, directly under the base stats. Equipment drops sometimes have hidden stats; bring the item to an appraising NPC to reveal them. Crafted (artisan) items often have substats. (For the list of appraising NPCs, see the forum's Appraisal reference.) `[IMAGE 2 — two base stats (Accuracy 37, Critical 22) with substats hidden under "Item Appraisal Required"]`

**ITEM GRADE** — every item now has an item grade. It doesn't reflect the item type (hat, wing, etc.) but has a big effect on moving substats. It's shown under the durability/type and above any base stats or substats.
- **Numeric** — grade 1 to grade 9. `[IMAGE 3 — item grade 9]`
- **Event** `[IMAGE 4 — event item grade]`
- **Unique** `[IMAGE 5 — unique item grade]`

NOTE: Costumes in the Item Mall no longer have stats or substats.

**BLANK ENCHANTMENT SCROLLS** — red scrolls available in the Valor Shop. You need one blank enchantment scroll for every substat you want to move.

**IMBUED ENCHANTMENT SCROLLS** — when a substat is moved from an item onto a Blank Enchantment Scroll, it becomes an Imbued Enchantment Scroll. You'll sometimes find Imbued Scrolls for sale in player shops.

## The key rule

The substats must come from an item that has the **same item grade** as the item you want to put them on. So a substat for an event item must come from another event item; same for a unique item. For numeric grades, you can put substats from a grade 1 item onto a grade 9 item, **but not the other way around**.

## The process

You need an item with the substats you want (correct item grade) and a Blank Enchantment Scroll. Tip: move the item with the substats to the first inventory space and clear others out of the way; switch to the costume tab so you don't accidentally click your accessories.

1. Double click the blank scroll in the consumables tab.
2. Go to the equipment tab and double click the item with the substats. A box warns the item will be destroyed — accept it. The item disappears and the substats are now on the imbued scroll in consumables.
3. Double click the imbued scroll in consumables.
4. Go to the equipment tab and double click the item you want the substats put on.

That's all there is to it. If you're starting with an Imbued Scroll bought from another player, just do steps 3 and 4 — but be extra careful the substats came from an item of the correct grade.

## Gems vs. substats

If you place a gem on an item that has a substat, the gem stats apply but the substats don't (they're not gone — remove the gem and the substats apply again). If you try to add a substat to a gemmed item, you'll get an error message telling you to remove the gem first. `[IMAGE — gem and sub error message]`

## Possible substats (PS)

There are something like 250 possible substats — too many to list — but here are the primary max subs:
- Strength +25
- Dexterity +25
- Intelligence +25
- Concentration +25
- Charm +40
- Sense +25
- All Stats +6
- Attack Power +40
- Defense +100
- Accuracy +100
- Magic Defense +100
- Dodge Rate +100
- Movement Speed +60
- Attack Speed 5%
- Critical +60
- HP Recovery Rate +12
- MP Recovery Rate +12
- Max HP +400
- Max MP +200

An item can have a primary substat and a secondary substat, but never two primary stat subs (like Str and Dex together). It IS possible to have a primary sub (top of the list) plus a secondary sub (bottom of the list). Max subs can only be crafted on grade 7, 8 or 9 items; generally, event items can't have max substats.
