The Turtle Guards are in El Verloon Desert  Be sure to only kill Turtle Guards
You can enter the Forest of Wisdom from Anima Lake


After you have entered the Forest of Wisdom, follow the path until you see an island in the water on the right with three bridges connected to it. Do not use any bridge; instead, turn to the left and head towards the center until you see a path in the middle of two hills. That big sleeping tree with a face is the Tree of Wisdom.


Walk towards the big tree and go into the water near this root, where the arrow is pointing. There will be a dialog message. Click on that and you've gotten the Twig of Wisdom that you need but you have also angered the Smoulies and a bunch of them spawn and start attacking. Use that Junon Order Return Scroll to get out of there fast!
