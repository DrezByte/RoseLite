# Encyclopedia for Evo Newbies 2.0

**Source:** https://forum.roseonlinegame.com/topic/963-encyclopedia-for-evo-newbies-20/
**Author:** HoneyBuns (posted November 30, 2022)

> Note: Originally posted ~7 years earlier on the Warpportal forum for Titan players, then updated and reposted. Many entries use collapsed "Spoiler" blocks (NPC/location lists); their contents are preserved below. Disclaimer from the OP: information was correct at time of posting but not everything was finalized, so some details may have changed since. Entries are in loose alphabetical order.

---

**ADVENTURER'S PLAINS** — New map and quests. (See Jobs.)

**AMMO** — Available from NPCs (see Weapons for specifics). Nice for new players: the lowest quality of each ammo type (cannon shells for launchers, bean bullets for guns, wooden arrows for bows) is free (0 zulie). All ammo has some weight. Ammo can also be crafted by an artisan.

**APPRAISAL** — Done by NPCs only (reveals hidden stats on dropped equipment for a small fee):
- Zant — [Gypsy Merchant] Mina
- El Verloon — [Gypsy Merchant] Methio
- Junon Polis — [Gypsy Merchant] Bellia
- Anima Lake — [Gypsy Merchant] Edone
- Selba Village — [Gypsy Merchant] Tobar
- Magic City of Eucar (Luna) — [Arumic Merchant] Pabel; [Ferrell Guild] Sergi
- Xita Refuge (Eldeon) — [Ferrell Guild Staff] Gilbert
- Desert City of Muris (Orlo) — [Gypsy Merchant] Nadeem
- Ancient Oasis Shrine (Orlo) — [Lojala Smith] Ekblovo

**ARMOR** — Monster drops; buy from NPC up to level 200; craftable by an artisan up to level 200 (see Reinforcing, Artisan); Valor Shop level 50–240 (weapons to 225); Honor Shop level 100–240 (weapons to 225); dungeon drops.
- *Junon NPCs:* Adv Plains — [Novice Designer] Cassirin (Visitor clothing to lvl 10, on far dock); Luxem — [Smith] Rockwell (back items lvl 8–22); Zant — [Designer] Keenu (Muse/Dealer armor lvl 10 & 30, back items lvl 8 & 18, recreational offhands) and [Armor Seller] Carrion (Soldier/Hawker armor lvl 10 & 30, backshields lvl 12 & 22, backpacks lvl 8 & 18); Breezy Hills — [Smith] Punwell (lvl 30 armor all classes, backshields lvl 22 & 32); Junon Polis — [Armor Merchant] Saki (Soldier/Hawker gear lvl 10–85, backshields lvl 12–42) and [Designer] Lisa (Muse/Dealer armor lvl 10–85, back items lvl 8 & 78, recreational offhands).
- *Luna NPCs:* Eucar — [Tavern Owner] Anzhelika (Soldier/Hawker armor lvl 100–120); [Arumic Merchant] Pabel (Muse/Dealer armor lvl 100–120).
- *Eldeon NPCs:* Xita Refuge — [Storage Keeper] Dustin Leta (armor lvl 130–160, all classes).
- *Orlo NPCs:* Desert City of Muris — [Armor Merchant] Azim (armor lvl 180–200 all classes, magic offhands int 200–240, Arun Plate Shield str 289); Golden Ring — [First Mate] Bighand Jack (armor lvl 180 all classes, Jabberwock Shield str 273, Eye of Storm offhand int 200).

**BUFFS** — All job classes now have four buffs shareable in a party or group (including muse — a big change):
- Muses: Magic Resistance Up, Max MP Up, Enhance Damage, Int buffs (plus a self-only enhance damage buff)
- Soldiers: Attack Power Up, Defense Up, Max HP Up, Strength buff
- Hawkers: Movement Speed Up, Dodge Up, Attack Speed Up, Dexterity buff
- Dealers: Accuracy Up, Critical Up, Concentration, Sense buffs

**CHARACTER LEVEL CAP** is 250.

**CLANS** — A new clan system, very different from old iROSE (see the clan guide).

**CLANFIELDS** — Junon Clanfield is restricted to characters level 30–89 who are Commander or higher (currently closed). Luna Clanfield can be entered by any character level 70+ in a clan (regardless of rank).

**COSTUME TAB** — Any gear can be used as a costume if you meet its requirements. Other players only see what's equipped on the costume tab.

**DRILLS** — Used to make sockets in equipment so gems can be placed.

**DRIVING** — Cart at lvl 50 (quest is slightly different, see that topic); Castle Gear at level 110 (see that topic).

**DROP NAMES** — Show by default. Change via System (in game) → Game Options.

**DROP SYSTEM** — Different from iROSE. It takes into account monster classification (guard, fighter, ranger, etc.). Some items drop from every monster on a map; higher-classification monsters can drop more uncommon materials/equipment on top of map drops; Kings have their own tier; some materials are planet drops (like Ice Shards on Luna). So each kill can yield several drops.

**DUELS** — See the duels topic.

**ELEMENTS** — Monsters, weapons, armor and skills can have an element, with a huge impact on PvM and PvP combat (see the Elements topic).

**ENCHANTMENT SCROLLS** — Blank Enchantment Scrolls remove substats from an item; Imbued Enchantment Scrolls carry substats taken from an item (see Substats).

**FORGOTTEN TEMPLE (Luna)** — No longer a PvP zone. Walk into the dark area near the NPC to enter.

**GAME ARENA** (Alt+G or G, same window as Group) — Join instances of PvP games and PvM dungeons via a queue; you're auto-added to the correct instance for your level range. NOTE: As of 2024-01-30, energy is no longer needed to use Game Arena.

**GAME OPTIONS** — The new client added many customization options. System → Game Options.

**GEMS** — Some crafted, some monster drops; also in Honor and Valor shops (some have level requirements). Every equipment item can be gemmed but no gem fits every piece — check the "Compatible Slot" on the gem. Crafted gems (Ruby, Garnet, etc.) go in accessories, weapons or the body armor part. For other gems the cut indicates the slot (credit to Zurn): Asscher — Foot; Baguette — Arms; Briolette — Face; Cushion — Body; Marquise — Weapon; Oval — Jewelry; Pear — Head; Round — Back; Trilliant — Shield. (See the gems topic.)

**GROUPS** (Alt+G or G → group side tab) — Used in dungeons and on regular maps. Groups share buffs and heals without being in the same party; no experience or drops are shared. Up to 15 people (including leader), and several parties can be in one group.

**INTERPLANETARY TRAVEL** — Luna (level 70, quest required); Eldeon (level 120); Orlo (level 200).

**ITEM MALL** — See the topic on buying ROSE points and the Item Mall window.

**JEWELRY (accessories)** — Have base stats but no sockets; can be drilled for a gem socket; can also take substats via an Imbued Enchantment Scroll of the correct item grade.
- Sold by: Zant — [Gypsy Merchant] Mina (lvl 20); El Verloon — [Gypsy Merchant] Methio (lvl 20); Junon Polis — [Gypsy Merchant] Bellia (lvl 50); Anima Lake — [Gypsy Merchant] Edone (lvl 50); Eucar (Luna) — [Ferrell Guild Banker] Andre (lvl 75). Monster drops for all jewelry above lvl 75; some craftable sets for lvl 100, 140, 160; Hiria's Tear necklace is a level 210+ quest reward (requires 220 to equip).

**JOBS** — 1st job at level 10 (no quest needed); 2nd job quest at level 100. Birth Island is gone; Adventurer's Plains is different. Talk to the fairy on first entry; she sends you to [Village Chief] Grey; follow the tutorial to Trainer Village to pick your first job. 2nd job quests are mostly as remembered, except the monsters needed all have "Supreme" in their names (Supreme Krawfy King, etc.) — easier to kill once you have the job quest.

**LUXEM** — New map and quests.

**MATERIALS FOR CRAFTING** — Sold by: Adv Plains — [Ferrell Guild Staff] Seyon; Zant — [Ferrell Guild Staff] Ulysses; Breezy Hills — [Ferrell Guild Staff] Belz; El Verloon — [Ferrell Guild Merchant] Lina; Eucar (Luna) — [Ferrell Guild Staff] Sergei; Xita Refuge (Eldeon) — [Storage Keeper] Dustin Leta.

**MINI MAP** — You can zoom in/out by clicking the minimap and using the mouse wheel.

**MOVEMENT** — Use a mouse, WASD keys, or a game controller.

**OBLIVION TEMPLE (OT)** — The big pyramid in El Verloon; a functioning map here (unused in iROSE). Minimum level 130; talk to [Archaeologist] Williams to enter (no entry fee now). Monster levels 145–170. [Ferrell Guild Merchant] Lina in El Verloon has repeatable OT quests (little village across the bridge from OT). NPCs on the main floor also give quests; monsters are on the lower levels (stairs in the center). The Halls of Oblivion daily-quest NPC is on OT's main floor.

**PARTIES** — Max 10 people (can have 10 in a level 1 party). To create a party, members must be within 19 levels of each other.

**PREMIUM SERVICE** — TBD.

**PREVIEW** — Ctrl + right-click an item to open a preview window; rotate your character and scroll to zoom. Works for Item Mall, NPCs, player shops, your inventory or storage.

**QUESTS** — Quest drops are shared in a party. Killing quests are generally worth doing and are repeatable — get quests on all characters (also applies to the 2nd job quest). See the quest-list topic.

**REINFORCEMENT** — Added ~1 year before NA closed (same name as the artisan crafting skill but different). Done by [Armor Merchant] Saki in Junon Polis; mostly end-game — increases item durability up to 100 via a refining-like window.
- Durability can increase on success or decrease on failure by up to 5 levels per attempt; failed items won't drop below 1 durability; items at 0 durability can't be reinforced. Materials drop from monsters in Game Arena dungeons: Reinforcement Powder (all durability levels); Reinforcement Alloy (Sn) up to 40; (Cr) up to 60; (Mn) up to 80; (Ti) up to 100.

**REPAIR HAMMERS / REPAIRING** — Repair hammers don't lower durability; different qualities repair different amounts; consumed on use. NPCs that sell hammers or repair: Adv Plains — [Smith] Ronk (also repairs); Luxem — [Smith] Rockwell (also repairs); Zant — [Weapon Seller] Raffle (repairs), [Armor Seller] Carrion (hammers); Breezy Hills — [Smith] Punwell (hammers); Junon Polis — [Armor Merchant] Saki (hammers), [Weapon Merchant] Crune (repairs); Selba Village — [Gypsy Merchant] Tobar (hammers), [Blacksmith] Brock (repairs); Eucar (Luna) — [Smith] Pavrick (both); Xita Refuge (Eldeon) — [Smith] Nel Eldora (both); Sikuku Ruins (Eldeon) — [Smith] Koij (repairs); Desert City of Muris (Orlo) — [Weapon Merchant] Azim (hammers), [Smith] Mahyr (repairs); Ancient Oasis Shrine (Orlo) — [Lojala Trader] Brizo (hammers), [Lojala Smith] Ekblovo (repairs); Golden Ring (Orlo) — [First Mate] Bighand Jack (hammers).

**REFINING** — Armor and weapons refine up to 20; done only by NPCs (not artisans). NOTE: the refine items (Bindrunes, Talisman, Apotrophe) are no longer sold by NPCs — they drop from monsters based on monster level and item grade of the material. Upgrade NPCs: Zant — [Weapon Seller] Raffle; Junon Polis — [Weapon Merchant] Crune; Breezy Hills — [Weapon Craftsman] Mairath; Selba Village — [Blacksmith] Brock; Eucar (Luna) — [Smith] Pavrick; Xita Refuge (Eldeon) — [Smith] Nel Eldora; Sikuku Ruins (Eldeon) — [Smith] Koji; Desert City of Muris (Orlo) — [Smith] Mahyr; Ancient Oasis Shrine (Orlo) — [Lojala Smith] Ekblovo. (See the refining topic.)

**REINFORCING (Artisan)** — An artisan skill that upgrades NPC armor/weapons. Reinforced armor gets a bit more def and extra HP bonus; endowing armor increases MP and MP recovery. Weapons: Darkening (extra attack speed), Hardening (extra attack power), Balancing (extra accuracy), Enchanting (reduced MP consumption).

**RESETS** — Three reset skills currently in the Item Mall skill tab (Stat Reset, Job Skill Reset, Unique Skill Reset). Location may change and a cooldown may be added, but resets are more available than in the past.

**SAVE TOWN** — Defaults to Adventurer's Plains; change your respawn as you progress. NPCs: Zant — [Junon Order Co-Founder] Francis, [Righteous Crusader] Leonard; El Verloon — [Ikaness Staff] Orias; Junon Polis — [Mayor] Darren; Eucar (Luna) — [Arumic Prophet] Olleck Basilasi; Xita Refuge (Eldeon) — [Junon Order Elder] Oscar Patrick.

**SCREENSHOTS** — Press Print Screen; find shots in the Screenshots folder of Pictures on your PC. For no-interface shots use Alt+U or /noui; press Escape (or /noui again) to bring the interface back.

**SCROLLS** — NPCs that sell food usually sell return scrolls if available:
- *Junon:* Adv Plains — [Little Street Vendor] Pony (1,000z); Zant — [Tavern Owner] Sharlin (3,000z, lvl 10); Junon Polis — [Tavern Owner] Harin (4,800z, lvl 30); Selba Village — [Shop Waitress] Betty (7,000z, lvl 80)
- *Luna:* Eucar — [Tavern Owner] Anzhelika (6,400z, lvl 70)
- *Eldeon:* Xita Refuge — [Chef] Peppie (8,000z, lvl 120); Shady Jungle — [Sikuku Resident] Shima (9,000z, lvl 140); Sikuku Underground Prison — [Herbalist] Helianthus (11,000z, lvl 160)
- *Orlo:* Desert City of Muris — [Orlo Bar Owner] Sahrazod (15,000z, lvl 200); Ancient Oasis Shrine — [Lojala Trader] Brizo (17,000z, lvl 200)

**SERVER TIME** — The clock under the mini map is server time; hover to see your local time.

**SKILL SYSTEM** (Alt+K or K) — 1 skill point per level starting at level 10; passives max at level 5 (10 points total); most active skills max at level 5 (5 points total).
- *Basic Skill Tab:* skills need no points — drag to the hot bar. Includes Ride Request (used once you can drive and have a backseat), Disassemble (any level, on equipment/weapons), Target Enemy, Target Ally (handy for healing clerics), Jump Skill (or space bar), Stone Throw (no rocks needed), and Despawn Summons.
- *Job Skill Tab:* after your first job, 3 more tabs appear; the Common Tab holds your 4 buffs plus other skills; the other two have passives and attack skills — check requirements and weapon type before spending points.
- *Parts Tab:* Drive Cart and Drive Castle Gear skills appear here once earned.
- *Unique Tab:* passive skills from certain quests, plus job-specific skills from unique skill books.
- *Item Mall Tab:* no skills now except the three reset skills.
- *Clan Tab:* clan skills if you're in a clan.
- After the 2nd job quest, a 4th skill tab appears; many of its skills require maxing skills from the other three tabs.

**STATS** — Max per stat is 425. Same stats (STR, DEX, INT, CON, SEN, CHA) but some functions changed: DEX no longer affects movement speed; CHA is needed for strong buffs (cleric and self buffs). To add stats faster, hold Shift when clicking the + to bring up a calculator for the total to add.

**STORAGE** — 4 tabs/sections (multi-page tabs use bottom arrows); extra account/character tabs buyable in the Item Mall.
- Account — shared by all characters (4 pages)
- Character — anything, but character-bound/locked items only go here (1 page)
- Item Mall — for IM purchases; expands as needed (1 page initially)
- Premium — TBD

**SUBSTATS** (light blue stats) — When the item is equipped on the Avatar tab, they apply to your character. Move substats between items of the same item grade with a Blank Enchantment Scroll (see Enchantment Scrolls; see the "how to move substats" topic).

**SUNSHINE COAST** — Enter from the upper level of Canyon City of Zant.

**SUMMONS** — Hold Ctrl and click a monster and your summons will attack it, even if you aren't. There's also a skill to despawn summons.

**TELEPORTATION TICKETS** — TBD.

**TRADE RESTRICTIONS** — Stated directly under the item name. Item Mall items are account locked (can't be sold/traded).
- Account bound/locked on equip or use — tradable/sellable until used or equipped; usable by any character on the account.
- Character bound/locked on equip or use — tradable/sellable until used or equipped; usable only by that character; only stored in the Character tab.
- Character locked on acquire — locked to the character as soon as it enters inventory; only stored in the Character tab.
- NOTE: bound/locked items can't be unbound/unlocked.

**VALOR AND HONOR** — Implemented when Union Wars were removed. Valor is PvM, Honor is PvP. Three PvP games and four PvM dungeons (see the Group window, Alt+G or G). Honor Points from PvP games; Valor Points from dungeons. A Battlemaster NPC on each planet gives PvP missions; there are daily dungeon quests plus repeatable Valor Point quests on all four planets. Points spend in the Valor/Honor Shops.

**VENDING** — Shop listings are now saved when you stop vending; click "reset" to change offerings. System → Game Options → Game Tab has options to hide all shop names or show which shops you've already viewed.

**WEAPONS** — Monster drops; buy from NPC up to level 200; craftable by an artisan up to level 200 (see Reinforcing, Artisan); Valor Shop level 50–225; Honor Shop level 100–225.
- *Junon NPCs:* Adv Plains — [Smith] Ronk (Visitor weapons lvl 5–10); Trainer Village — job-specific weapons (lvl 10); Luxem — [Weapon Merchant] Dune (all classes lvl 10–20); Zant — [Arumic Merchant] Tryteh (magic weapons/offhands lvl 10–40), [Eccentric Inventor] Spero (guns/launchers lvl 10–48, ammo), [Weapon Seller] Raffle (all other weapons lvl 10–40, arrows); Breezy Hills — [Smith] Punwell (weapons/ammo all classes); El Verloon — [Ferrell Guild Merchant] Lina (ammo, all weapons); Junon Polis — [Weapon Merchant] Crune (Soldier/Hawker weapons lvl 47–80, arrows, offhand shields str 52–99), [Arumic Merchant] Chelsie (magic weapons lvl 47–81, magic offhands int 50–80).
- *Luna NPCs:* Eucar — [Arumic Merchant] Pabel (magic weapons lvl 88–117), [Smith] Pavrick (Soldier/Hawker weapons lvl 88–118, arrows), [Ferrell Guild Staff] Sergei (guns/launchers lvl 88–117, bullets & shells).
- *Eldeon NPCs:* Xita Refuge — [Smith] Nel Eldora (Soldier/Hawker/Muse weapons lvl 123–155, arrows), [Ferrell Guild Staff] Gilbert (guns/launchers lvl 123–155, arrows, bullets & shells); Shady Jungle — [Sikuku Resident] Shima (arrows, bullets & shells); Sikuku Underground Prison — [Herbalist] Helianthus (arrows, bullets & shells).
- *Orlo NPCs:* Desert City of Muris — [Weapon Merchant] Huzam (Soldier/Hawker/Muse weapons lvl 175–200), [Gunsmith] Rahd (bows, guns, crossbows, launchers lvl 175–200, ammo); Golden Ring — [First Mate] Bighand Jack (weapons lvl 175 all classes, ammo); Ancient Oasis Shrine — [Lojala Trader] Brizo (ammo).

**ZANT** — Map is a bit different and NPCs are in different spots. Access Sunshine Coast from the upper level of Zant.
