## Staff bolter advantage vs wand auto attack on early levels

- Skills scale faster than auto attacks because muse don't get a lot of buff for auto attack while skills get multiple buffs early
- Sorcery skills (ManaBolt/Jolt) tend to have higher damage since most lower-level mobs are neutral
- Lower Int requirement (Cursed Staff) — you can equip it a lot earlier than the Unique wand
- Higher weapon atk
- Easier to switch from single-target bolter to AOE build

***Since the Devs decided to make unique weapons harder to obtain, the price of Cursed Staff in a few months might not be newbie friendly. A good alternative is the level 45 **Stave of the Estoric Order**, a reward after completing the level 40 armor quest. Minimal atk difference but each weapon has +8% pvm damage (see the 36–50 armor quest spoiler for which quest rewards it).

Stave of the Estoric Order vs Cursed Staff — [IMAGE: staff comparison](https://media.invisioncic.com/i318093/monthly_2025_07/Staff.jpg.89cb7be4fa2d1364293593606a1c9dca.jpg)

**\*NOTE\*** There is a huge difference between the accuracy of the Cursed Staff and the Stave of the Estoric Order. If you only use the quest-reward weapon, buy the level 20 Accuracy accessory set, then switch to level 50 and level 75 accessories when you hit those levels.

## Leveling Quests / Spots

### 1–20 — Follow the quest to Zant for a free Blue armor set and free Purple weapon

**Adventurer's Plain**
1. Talk to Arua Fairy
2. Speak to Village Chief Gray — defeat 5 Mini Jelly Beans
3. Delivery for Bert
4. Help Bert Clear the Road — defeat Mother Choropy and Choropies
5. Speak to Huey
6. Help Huey Collect Flanae Seeds — kill Flanaes
7. Collect Honey for the Farmer — kill Young Honeybee
8. Collect Pumpkin Seeds — kill Pumpkins
9. Visit Training Village — go here at lvl 10 to change into 1st Job

**Valley of Luxem Tower**
10. Travel to Luxem Tower — speak to Morgan
11.1 Missing Cart Parts (young pomic)
11.2 A Small Start (red pomic) — Reward: Blue Hat of choice *(choose the armor with MP Recovery)*
11.3 Collecting Ore (red pomic) — Reward: Blue Gloves of choice *(choose MP Recovery)*
11.4 Honey for her Honey — Reward: Blue Shoes of choice *(choose MP Recovery)*
12. Delivery for Rudolfo — Reward: Blue Armor of choice *(choose MP Recovery)*
13. Speak to Lutis

**Zant**
14. Speak to Lena
15. Honeybee Helper — defeat Hornets
16. Welcome to Zant (in order): 16.1 Talk to Crow, 16.2 Mina, 16.3 Raffle, 16.4 Ulysses, 16.5 Raffles, 16.6 Crow, 16.7 Sharlin, 16.8 Go back to Crow to get your first **Free Purple Weapon — choose the staff.** (Be careful clicking, there's no confirmation.)
17. Meet the chief (Cornell)

After getting the weapon you can just drop quest 17, then focus on getting the **Junon mask** by following the Hero Quest. You can store it, so if you already have it on another character it's easier. This is your mask even at end game.

The Hero Quest pops up at level 15 starting from the *Message from Arua* quest. Follow HoneyBuns' guide until you get the mask, then drop it (the rest isn't very rewarding, so most people stop at the mask):
- **[Guide] Episode Quest aka Hero Quest:** https://forum.roseonlinegame.com/topic/6089-guide-episode-quest-aka-hero-quest/#comment-24144

### 20–36 — Aqua Guard repeatable quest
Williams (El Verloon) — 35k exp, 39k zulie

### 36–50 — Chain quest → EXP armor set and level 45 Purple weapon
You can start the quest at level 32, but as a newbie it's better to farm the zulie quest from aquas until lvl 36.

**El Verloon Desert**
1. Get the quest from Caraan
2. Meet the Locals: 2.1 Orias, 2.2 Methio, 2.3 Lina, 2.4 back to Caraan, 2.5 Franklin
3. Rambunctious Rackie Problem: 3.1 defeat Rackies, 3.2 speak to Linda
4.1 Beetle Surprise (kill any beetle)
4.2 Turtle Soup (kill any turtle)
4.3 Freaky Fishmen Souffle (kill any aqua)
5. Missing My Sweetie — speak to Rick

*Parts 6 and 7 can be hard for newbies; if you're 38+ you can go straight to Stephano on Anima Lake and follow #9+*
6. Missing My Wallet — kill Bees and Hornets near
7. Take Down the Queens — kill Queen Bee workers
8. A Nice Place to Visit — speak to Stephano

**Anima Lake**
9. Talk to Stephano: 9.1 Bash to Bacon Bits (kill Mini Porkies), 9.2 Highway Porkery (kill Porkies)
10. Making the Roads Safe Again: 10.1 kill Crazy Porkies and Porkie Hooligans, 10.2 speak to Canterbury — Reward: **Enigmatic Vestments** (Exp armor part)
11. Fishy Smelling Equipment Collection: 11.1 kill specific Aquas, 11.2 speak to Canterbury
12. The Anima Lake Horror: 12.1 kill specific Aquas, 12.2 speak to Canterbury — Reward: **Enigmatic Hat** (Exp armor part)
13. A Visit to Edone — speak to Edone
14.1 Grunter Axe Collection / 14.2 Grunter Raid / 14.3 Taking out the Grunter Leaders
15. The Great Gorilla Escape: 15.1 kill Wild Gorilla, Porkie Hooligans and Grunters, 15.2 speak to Edone — Reward: **Enigmatic Sandals** and **Enigmatic Gloves** (Exp armor parts)

**Junon**
16. To the Big City at Last — speak to Winters
17. A Miner Problem — kill Moldies
18. Welcome to Junon
19. The Saga Continues — speak to Darren — Reward: **Level 45 Purple Weapon**
