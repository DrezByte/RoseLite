This guide is for the long quest that begins on Junon Planet and ends (for now) on Eldeon Planet. You can complete this quest at any time, even after you are max level.  The quest has many parts, and most of the parts have several stages. Info about each quest can be found in the spoiler below the name of the quest.

Four very important things to remember:
1- Some parts of this quest require more than one "quest slot" so be sure that you don't have more than 8 open quests when doing the Hero quest.

2- If you abandon any of the quests, try to remember which part you were on and talk to the NPCS involved and you should be able to get the quest back. If you can not get the quest back after doing this please submit a ticket here

3- Whenever you see a flashing bell on the left side of your game screen like this one, do NOT ignore it!  Click on it right away, even if you aren't planning to work on the Hero quest at that time. Once ignored, it can take days to reappear and sometimes not even then, so save yourself much aggravation and click on it as soon as you see it.

4- If the bell does not show up when it is supposed to while you are doing the quest, close the game completely (not select character) and log back in, it should pop up. You may need to give it a few minutes after closing the game before logging back in.

Spoiler

Junon's Holy Staff (1)
Exit Zant from the middle and the Breezy Hills entrance will be directly in front of you.


Breezy Hills entrance


Once you are in Breezy Hills, head to the bridge. When you get to the cross-bridge, turn to the right. You can see the town up on the hill.


Junon's Holy Staff (2)
Pomic Fighters are in Breezy Hills. Beetles are in El Verloon around the water.


Pomic Fighter


To get to El Verloon Desert safely, head back across the bridges the same way you first crossed them to get to the town. From this point, go straight across that bridge in the distance and keep going straight


El Verloon entrance


Once you are in El Verloon Desert, the Beetles are to the left, on the far side of the map, but getting there can be both confusing and dangerous, depending on your level. So here is a shortcut way to get to the right area.

If you look to your right, you can see part of the big pyramid called Oblivion Temple. 


Make your way over to that pyramid, then go around to the back of it. You will see a circular brick area and to the side, past the waterfall, there is a bridge.


Cross that bridge and then you can jump down from the cliff at any point.  The lower level Beetles are around the shoreline.  To return to Francis once you have the items from the Pomics and Beetles, you can retrace your steps or use a Zant Return Scroll.
