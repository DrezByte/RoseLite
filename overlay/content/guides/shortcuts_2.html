Movement & Interaction

W / A / S / D – Move your character

Spacebar – Jump

/ – Toggle between running and walking

Tab – Target or switch between enemies

T – Loot nearby dropped items

Z – Sit down

Alt (Hold) – Temporarily display item names on the ground

Alt + Ctrl – Permanently display item names on the ground

Ctrl + Left Click on Monster – Command summons to attack the selected monster

Alt + Left Click on NPC – Interact with NPCs even if players are blocking them

Interface & Menus

I – Open Inventory

C – Open Character window

K – Open Skills window

J – Open Quest log

N – Open Clan window

M – Open Map

H – Open Group window

G – Open Game Arena

B – Open Item Mall

O – Open Community window

Y – Open Game Options

Esc – Open System Menu

Alt + U – Toggle UI visibility

Targeting

F1 – Target yourself (normal mode)

F2 – Target the next player (normal mode)

F3 – Target the previous player (normal mode)

Mouse & Modifier Keys

Middle Mouse Button – Move forward

Shift + Left Click (on item) – Quickly move items to storage or NPC window

Shift + Left Click (on stat) – Add a custom amount of stat points

Ctrl + Right Click (on item) – Preview item appearance on your character

Chat Emotes & Commands

Use these in chat to trigger animations or emotes:

/sit – Sit down

/laugh, lol, rofl – Laugh

/omg, omfg – Shocked expression

/wtf, /angry, /mad – Show anger

/cheer, huzzah – Cheer

/bye, goodbye, cya – Wave goodbye

/hi, hello, howdy – Greet

/thanks, thx, ty – Express gratitude

/clap, /applaud – Applaud

/kiss, – Show affection

/bow, /curtsy – Perform a bow or curtsy

/kick – Kick animation

Additional Tips

Alt Bar Hotkeys: Enable the Alt hotkey bar via game settings to assign skills/items to the number keys 1 through =.

Stat Allocation Shortcut: Hold Shift and click a stat in the character window to allocate a custom amount.

Item Preview: Hold Ctrl and right-click an item to preview how it looks on your character.
