This feature was added to the game in April, 2018 before the old official server closed.  Don't be confused because the name is the same as the artisan skill.

 Item Reinforcement gives players the ability to raise the durability of their armor and weapons to 100. Increasing the durability adds to the dodge of armor and the accuracy of weapons. Items with 0 durability are considered damaged beyond repair and can not be reinforced, so be sure to repair your item first.

THE BASICS
-The materials needed are all drops in the Game Arena Dungeons.

-All reinforcement attempts require Reinforcement Powder. The quantity you need varies, depending on the current durability of the item. You will need Reinforcement Powder for each attempt.


-The current level of the item determines which alloy is needed. 
Reinforcement Alloy (Sn)  Used to reinforce items up to 40 durability

Reinforcement Alloy (Cr)  Used to reinforce items up to 60 durability

Reinforcement Alloy (Mn)  Used to reinforce items up to 80 durability

Reinforcement Alloy (Ti)  Used to reinforce items up to 100 durability


-Currently, there are no Reinforcement Enhancers in the game. If that changes in the future, I will update this guide accordingly.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To increase the durability of your gear, talk to [Armor Merchant] Saki in Junon Polis.  Choose option 2 


When the reinforcement window opens, drag your item to the empty space at the top. The needed materials will be shown below the icon of the item. The zulie cost for the attempt is directly under the materials, and the success chance is at the bottom of the window.  


When you drag the required materials to the reinforcement window, the risk level information lights up. 


There are three risk levels to choose from. 
No Risk- If the reinforcement attempt fails, there will be no loss of durability. 
Medium Risk (+15%)= If the reinforcement attempt fails, the current durability of the item will decrease
High Risk (+30%)- If the reinforcement attempt fails, the durability will decrease and there is a chance that the item will be destroyed



If you place your cursor over the item's icon in the Reinforced Item section of the window, you will see the current durability and the possible durability gains based on which risk level has been chosen.
No Risk


Medium Risk The number in red is what the durability will be if the attempt fails


High Risk If the reinforcement attempt fails, the item will be destroyed




Regardless of which risk level, if an attempt fails, you will lose the materials (and the zulie of course). Note that as the durability level increases, so does the cost in zulie and the success chance is reduced. This is a pretty expensive process, and while you can reinforce any gear, most players only reinforce their end game gear.
