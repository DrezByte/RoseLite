Mounts are a fun way to get around ingame. You do not lose buffs when riding a mount like you do when driving a cart. However, you CAN be knocked off your mount if you’re attacked by a monster.  Mounts will be account locked when used and some have level restrictions. If no level restriction is listed, that means you can use the mount at any level. All mounts have 1500 movement speed.  If your clan has the Stable Training passive, your mount will be faster. 

Here are the different ways to get a mount. Check the spoilers to see how the mounts look in the game. There are screen shots with a character riding and ss of just the mount itself.
