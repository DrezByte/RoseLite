Seems like only yesterday we were having fun at the Summer Festival on Sunshine Coast.  Now it's time to get back into the classroom. Hope you studied because exams are already here.   During this event, monsters around your level will sometimes drop School Spirit. More about that later.

Talk to [Event Info] Judy in Zant to get started. You should do them in order (first grade, second grade, etc). Make sure you have space in your equipment tab for 6 items and that you do not have more than 9 open quests.  I know that not everyone wants to see the answers. If you get stuck, check the spoiler below each exam.

FIRST GRADE
Counting Mini Jelly Beans. Judy will give you a math equation to solve, then you have to kill that number of Mini Jellybeans. 

Spoiler

Be sure that you only kill the Mini Jellybeans.  The correct answer is 7

Return to Judy and if you have killed the correct number of beans, she will give you the next exam

 

SECOND GRADE
Pumpkin Patch Math. Another math problem to solve and then go kill that number of pumpkins.

Spoiler

As before, be sure to only kill regular pumpkins. The correct answer is 13.

Return to Judy again and if you killed the correct number of pumpkins, Judy will give you the next exam.

THIRD GRADE
Turtles Divided. This time you'll be heading to El Verloon once you figure out the answer.

Spoiler

Only regular turtles will count. The correct answer is 14.

Head back to Judy again with the correct number to get the next exam.

FOURTH GRADE
This Little Mini Porkie.  Now it's time to go to Anima Lake and hunt down the Mini Porkies.

Spoiler

Only Mini Porkies will count. The correct answer is 25.

Return to Judy again. If you collected the correct number of piggies, she will give you the next exam.

 

FIFTH GRADE
Scientific Monster Names. Finally an exam that is not math-related!  Judy will give you the names of three monsters but the letters in the names are jumbled up. Figure out what the real names are and then go kill one of each of those mobs.

Spoiler

These are kind of tricky if you are not good at word scramble games. Here are the correct monsters and their locations.

Optical Maiden – Captain Moldie- outside of Junon Polis
Iced Oyster Plum – Decrepit Smouly- Forest of Wisdom
Dear Leg Turner- Grunter Leader-Anima Lake
 

Back to Judy once again to get the next exam.
