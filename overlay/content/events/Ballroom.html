Dust off your dancing shoes,   the Ballroom event has started.

YOU NEED TO BE LEVEL 75 OR HIGHER TO GET THE COSTUMES

-Lacrima is the main ingredient in this event, and it drops everywhere from monsters that are near your level. No clue what Lacrima is or why she wants it but once you have gathered some, talk to [Event Info] Felice Fete in Junon Polis and she will teleport you to the Grand Ballroom.  You will need one Lacrima each time you enter, not just the first time.

To leave the Ballroom, just walk out of this door

-Felice's sister, [Event Info] Lucille Fete, is inside the ballroom and she sells dance scrolls and a firecracker. When you buy a dance scroll, you actually get 2 scrolls- 1 (F) for female characters and 1 (M) for male characters. If you time it right with a friend (or your second character), it will look like they are actually dancing together. Characters with high charm will get more than one of each per purchase.

-When enough Lacrima is spent with Lucille (by buying firecrackers and the dance scrolls), the Grand Duke will spawn in the center of Junon Polis, near Felice.   Unlike the monsters during the Halloween Invasion, this guy is not at all aggressive. Whoever kills him will get the drop, a Golden Crystal.  Felice will exchange that for a True Hero's Emblem but you can also just buy one (check in the spoiler).
  

Spoiler

 If you have at least 6 Lacrima in your inventory and talk to Felice two times (without accepting her offer to teleport you to the Ballroom), she will have an option to buy a True Hero's Emblem for 5 Lacrima.


 


When the Grand Duke spawns, inside the Ballroom, the Gate Crashers will spawn. More about them in a minute.
NOTE: No one can be teleported into the Ballroom when there is a Grand Duke spawned in the city.

-If you talk to Lucille with the True Hero's Emblem and at least 210 Lacrima in inventory, she will have the option of a special gift. That gift is the Felice Party Wear.  NOTE: There are no shoes, hat or gloves for this set.

 

- To get the Lucille Party Wear, you must first buy 10 of each item in Lucille's shop. After that, when you ask to see her shop, you will find two new options The Robot Dance scrolls for 20 Lacrima each, and option 6 (be sure you have enough Lacrima in inventory)



To get the Leopold Sets, you need to get a quest.

Put the Felice Party Wear on the AVATAR tab (not costume) and talk to Lucille again and pick option 6.

She will recognize that you are wearing Felice Party Wear and give you the quest "Dance Your Best" . The Gate Crashers look like the Grand Duke, only smaller and not shiny.

-If there are not already Gate Crashers in the Ballroom, you can either wait until someone else buys stuff from Lucille or you can do it.  The amount seems to vary, but once you see them in that room, quickly go kill 10 then talk to Lucille again (with at least 210 Lacrima in inventory).

NOTE: you will have to do repeat this each time to get a Leopold set.
