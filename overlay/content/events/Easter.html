There is a lot to do in this event.  Let's begin in Zant.

Talk to [Event Info] Judy to start the Great Easter Egg Hunt.
NOTE: In order to complete this quest, you need to be able to travel to Luna (level 70)

Basically you have to find 6 missing Easter Eggs- All of them are different colors and all of them are on different maps. There are 10 locations on each map. When someone defeats an egg, it will respawn in one of the other 9 spots on that map so get ready to run around... a lot!
Judy wants you to return them to her in a specific order. This is going to take awhile to finish because each time you turn in eggs, she takes ALL of them so you have to get the quest again to get another prize To get every prize you have to do the quest 6 times.

Part of the fun of this event is finding the eggs but to help with that, check in the spoiler for the approximate locations of the eggs on each of the maps plus a picture of one of the eggs so you have an idea of what to look for.

Here are the rewards:

1 Red Egg = Silver, Calico, Brown or Golden Rabbit Headband
1 Red and 1 Yellow Egg = Easter Basket (offhand)
1 Red, 1 Yellow, 1 Green Egg = Easter Bunny Body
1 Red, 1 Yellow, 1 Green, 1 Blue Egg = Easter Bunny Paws
1 Red, 1 Yellow, 1 Green, 1 Blue, 1 Orange Egg = Easter Bunny Feet
1 Red, 1 Yellow, 1 Green, 1 Blue, 1 Orange Egg and 1 Purple Egg – Easter Bunny Head

If you get all 6 eggs then turn them in while wearing the full Easter Bunny Suit ON THE AVATAR TAB (head, body, gloves, shoes and Easter basket), pick option 9. Judy will recognize that you are wearing the full suit and will offer either a Special Easter Egg or a Rabbit Backpack.

This Special Easter Egg will contain one of the following:
Rabbit Headband as listed above (these do not have base stats but they might have substats)
Rabbit Backpack
Easter Egg Head
Easter Basket
10 Red Easter Eggs (HP restorative)
10 Blue Easter Eggs (MP restorative)
Easter Egg pet capsule (2)
Easter Bunny pet capsule (1)
Easter Egg (3) (HP restorative)
Easter Bunny Wings

In addition to the Easter Egg Hunt, [Mayor] Darren in Junon Polis needs some help with a problem.  Make sure you have less than 10 active quests, then speak to him to get the first part of a series of quests. 

NOTE: Due to coding issues and quest tracker limitations, when you complete the first two parts of this questliine (Slowing the Porkie Production and Pushing the Grunters Aside), it will appear as if the quest tracker has reset back to 0. Don't panic, just go back to Darren and he will give you the next quest. 

"Slowing the Porkie Production"  Defeat 200 Regular Porkies (they can be found in Anima Lake) and return to Darren with a scrap of paper.

"Pushing the Grunters Aside"  Defeat 200 Regular Grunters (there are some outside of Junon Polis to the left and more in Anima Lake). Once again, return to Darren with another scrap of paper

NOTE: When you get the third quest in this questline, the quest tracker will show that you have killed 2/3 Grunter Kings. Again, this is a coding/quest tracker issue.

Sadly, Darren realizes that you haven't killed any kings yet. Once you DO defeat a Grunter King, the quest tracker will turn green and tell you to let Darren know

"Show That King Who's Bacon... I Mean Boss" Defeat 1 Grunter King (again, outside of Junon Polis and in Anima Lake).  Return to Darren with guess what... another scrap of paper.

"Ask Mildun About the Scraps of Paper"   Talk to [Ferrell Guild Merchant] Mildun with those three scraps of paper and get one of the best rewards in the game- a Pig Cart! But you aren't finished yet.

"Talk to Darren" Mildun sends you back to the mayor.

"Uncovering the Secret" Now it's time to kill some more kings,  Check the spoiler below if you need help figuring out which ones.  You will get a tiny scrap of paper from each king. Return to the mayor when you have all 8 tiny scraps of paper.
