There is no quest for this event but it's super easy to participate. I call it the My Little Pony event and you'll see why in a minute. 

Magical Boxes of Friendliness can drop from monsters around your level.  They will go into the consumable tab of your inventory. When you open one of them, the item will go into either equipment, consumable, or material, depending on what it is so make sure that you have space in all three inventory tabs before you start opening boxes.

Here is what you can get from these magical boxes:
Honey (Red Jellybean Stew) - Consumable-Restores 50% of max HP over 10 seconds NOTE: Can not be used in Training Ground
Blue Jellybean Stew- Consumable-Restores 50% of max MP over 10 seconds.  NOTE can not be used in Training Ground
Magical Coupon- Material-Take these to [Designer] Keenu in Zant and you can exchange them for costume parts.
Delightful Costume Box- Consumable until opened, then Equipment.  Each of these boxes will contain a head or tail item.
Magical Box of Friendliness-Another box like the one you just opened.

Here are the choices when you exchange the Magical Coupons with Keenu. 


None of these hats and tails have base stats, but they can have substats and those substats could be moved to other event grade items.  There are six styles. Check the spoilers to see how they look in the game.
