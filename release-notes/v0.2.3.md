## What's new

### The overlay sticks to the game (Windows)
- The panel is now attached to the ROSE window itself. It stays above the game, follows the window straight away when you move it, and hides while the game is minimized.
- Switching to another app no longer makes the panel disappear and pop back: it goes behind that app together with the game.

### Collapsed menu
- When there's no room beside the game, the menu now waits as a small RoseLite logo on the game's edge. Click it, or rest the cursor on it for a moment, to open it. Moving the cursor past the edge no longer opens it by accident.
- Press Esc to close it.

### Navigation
- Esc now goes back: from an item page to the list, and from a section to the home screen. Esc won't take you back while a search box still has text in it.

## Fixes
- Copy buttons work again: item links, shop locations and shouts. They stopped copying in 0.2.2.

## Accessibility
- If you've turned off animation effects in Windows, RoseLite now turns off its animations too.
- The selected tile no longer pulses.
- Sections that need live game data are now marked as unavailable for screen readers, not just dimmed.

## Updating
Already installed? RoseLite downloads this update in the background the next time you start it, then offers to restart. If you choose Later, it installs when you close the app.
