Offline reference tools, organized beside the game

Browse items and monsters, plan crafting, mark quest progress, time kings, and open related records without leaving the companion.

Item Database

Search more than 19,000 items, inspect available stats, and follow links to NPC sources, recipes, and monsters that drop them.

King Respawn Timers

Tap when one of 100 kings is defeated, then sort the offline countdown board by zone or next spawn.

Market Board

When online, compare current listings, trending items, and 7-, 30-, or 90-day price history from the configured market service.

Quest Progress

Browse quest chains, filter by level and planet, review objectives and rewards, and mark completed quests.

Crafting & Gems

Browse recipes by craft skill and expand gem upgrades into a shopping list that accounts for lower ranks you own.

Built by adventurers, for adventurers

RoseLite is built in the open and requires no RoseLite account or sign-in. Progress stays on this PC, and Settings can export it to a file you carry yourself. Market prices, news and update checks require an internet connection; the bundled catalogs, guides, timers, and saved progress remain local.
