Feel bored of the existing casual effect? Replaces the default damage numbers with a hand-drawn comic style — damage dealt, damage received, blocks, absorbs and heals each get their own look.

Video preview ↗

File usages

  • digitnumber01 — damage given to enemy
  • digitnumber02 — damage received from enemy
  • digitnumber03 — blocked damage given to enemy
  • digitnumber04 — blocked damage received from enemy
  • digitnumber05 — absorbed damage received from enemy
  • digitnumber06 — heal amount received from ally/self
